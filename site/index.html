Censor Audio

Mutes profanity in anything you hear on your Mac — music, videos, streams — in real time, before it reaches your speakers.

Download for macOS

Apple Silicon · macOS 14.4+ · ~430 MB

  1. Unzip and drag Censor Audio to your Applications folder.
  2. Open it, and allow Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording when asked.
  3. Click the ear icon in the menu bar and switch it on.
Runs entirely on-device. No audio leaves your Mac.
